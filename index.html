FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Galmet — the party app, video chat app, and social platform in one.

What is Galmet?

Galmet is a social live entertainment app where you can join live party rooms, make 1-on-1 video calls, chat instantly, send virtual gifts, complete daily tasks for rewards, and connect with people around the world. It's designed for real-time social interaction — not just scrolling feeds.

Is Galmet a party app or a video chat app?

Both. Galmet offers multi-seat live party rooms (like a voice and video party app) and private HD video calls (like a dedicated video chat app). You also get direct messaging, a social friends graph, virtual gifts, and daily reward tasks — making it a full social media and entertainment platform.

What can I do in Galmet party rooms?

Host or join live rooms with up to 8 video/audio seats. Choose room categories like Chat, Dating, Sing, Make Friends, or Game. Send virtual gifts, use in-room chat, manage viewers, and minimize your room to keep browsing while staying connected.

How do virtual gifts and coins work?

Coins are used for video calls, virtual gifts, and in-app features. You can top up coins via Google Play. When you send gifts in chat, calls, or party rooms, creators earn gems. Verified creators can withdraw gems through a secure payout flow.

Can I earn money on Galmet?

Yes — creators can earn gems from receiving virtual gifts and video calls. After face verification, gems can be withdrawn. Daily tasks also reward coins and gems for staying active, taking calls, and participating in parties.

Is Galmet safe to use?

Galmet includes face verification for creators, block and report tools, screen protection on video calls, and community safety rules. Privacy Policy and User Agreement are available at galmetapp.com. You control what you share on your profile.

Is Galmet free to download?

Yes. Galmet is free to download on Android. Optional in-app coin purchases are available for calls, gifts, and premium interactions. New users may receive welcome coin rewards.

When will Galmet be available on Google Play?

Galmet is launching soon on Google Play. Visit galmetapp.com for the latest updates, or tap the Google Play button above to check availability.
