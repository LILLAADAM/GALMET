Galmet Child Safety Standards

Effective date: May 8, 2026

This page describes how Galmet – Talk, Chat & Fun (“Galmet”, “we”, “our”, “us”) addresses child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and related risks. It is published for users, parents, and platform partners.

Compliance and reporting to authorities

Galmet complies with applicable child-safety laws. Where required, we report to regional and national authorities in line with legal obligations. For general guidance on reporting obligations, refer to your local law enforcement and official resources. Google Play developers can consult Google’s documentation in the Google Play Help Center for policy and program requirements.

What we prohibit

Galmet strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation of minors
  • Grooming or any abusive behavior directed at minors
  • Any content or conduct that endangers children

In-app reporting

Users can report child safety concerns and other violations directly inside the Galmet app. Reports are taken seriously and may be used to investigate accounts and content.

Enforcement

Reported content and accounts may be reviewed. We may remove content, restrict features, or permanently ban accounts when violations are confirmed or when necessary to protect users and comply with the law.

Law enforcement

Galmet cooperates with law enforcement where legally required and when requests are valid under applicable law.
