Account & data deletion

This page is for users of the Galmet mobile app as listed on Google Play. Developer inquiries use the same contact channel below. For general privacy terms, see our Privacy Policy.

checklist

How to request deletion

  1. Open Galmet and sign in with the account you want us to act on.
  2. Go to Profile → Edit profile. Find User ID and tap Copy (you will paste it in the form).
  3. On this page, choose either full account deletion or deletion of specific data only (your account stays open).
  4. Complete the form using an email address we can reach you on, confirm the prompts, and submit.
  5. We will confirm receipt and follow up by email, usually within 7–14 business days. Full removal may take longer where backups or providers need processing (often completed within up to 30 days).

Full account deletion — typically deleted or anonymized

  • check Profile fields (nickname, bio, gender, age/DOB, region/country, photo)
  • check Chats and related stored content you sent (text, images, gifts metadata)
  • check Stored uploads (e.g. profile and chat images in our storage)
  • check In-app activity tied to your account (tasks, coins/gems/gifts history, notifications token)
  • check Call-related records associated with your account where technically feasible

May be retained for a limited time

Some information may be kept where required for legal, tax, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or abuse investigations, or as aggregated/anonymized data that does not identify you. Retention length depends on the category and obligation; we aim to complete deletion of routine personal data within up to 30 days after your identity is verified unless a longer period applies by law.

Partial data deletion — what we remove

We delete or anonymize the categories you select that apply to your Galmet account. Narrow requests may leave other data unchanged so the app can keep working.

What we may keep

Any category you do not ask us to delete may remain. We may also retain minimal records under the same legal / security / fraud exceptions above. Processing targets up to 30 days after verification unless a different timeline applies.
